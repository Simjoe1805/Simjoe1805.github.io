Nusantara AI Institute

Vice Chancellor  ·  Indonesia

Nusantara AI Institute (NAII) is one of Indonesia's leading AI learning and development platforms, dedicated to strengthening talent and capability across the country's growing AI ecosystem. The institute brings together education, industry, and policy to build a generation of AI-ready professionals.

As Vice Chancellor, I support the strategic direction of programs and institutional development — ensuring that academic priorities, talent outcomes, and operational execution remain aligned with the institute's long-term mission of democratizing AI literacy across Indonesia.

The role has included involvement in major national AI literacy initiatives, including programs that received featured coverage from Bloomberg Technoz and engagement from prominent national figures.
